FAQs

Questions before a demo

FinStarter is a white-label, production-ready foundation for digital investment platforms. Investment managers, brokers and fintechs start with what every firm otherwise builds from scratch - client portal with digital onboarding and KYC, portfolio and model management, analytics and reporting - and get a branded platform configured to their business in months, not years.

Compliance is built in, not bolted on: a tamper-evident audit trail with configurable retention, role-based access control and orchestrated KYC and sanctions screening give your firm the evidence regulators ask for. The regulatory obligations stay yours - FinStarter gives you the means to meet them.

FinStarter is built for firms that manage and report on portfolios for their own clients:

  • Discretionary investment managers and RIAs - modernise the client platform without a multi-year build;
  • Brokers - add a branded managed or advisory offering on top of existing execution;
  • Fintechs, digital banks and EMIs - embed an investment layer into your own product;
  • Firms running segregated mandates - give each mandate client a clear view of their portfolio;
  • Private client divisions - a dedicated client and operational layer on top of the existing core;
  • Financial publishers and research advisories - turn subscribers into managed clients.

If your firm has a direct relationship with the investors whose portfolios you run, FinStarter is designed for you.

It depends on how much you need to change.

  • FinStarter as is - about one week. We deploy the platform in your environment, apply your branding and configure the standard modules. You get a working platform you can use right away.
  • Extended to your requirements - typically 2-6 months. When you need custom functionality, specific workflows or integrations with your custodian, OMS or data providers, we agree the scope and timeline with you individually.

In both cases you start from a ready foundation rather than building from scratch - which is what usually takes firms 18-24 months.

FinStarter implementation costs depend on the solution scope and the level of customization required. An as-is implementation starts at approximately $10,000. More complex requirements, such as additional integrations or custom functionality, increase the cost.

You are not paying only for features. FinStarter comes with the controls regulators expect a firm to evidence - a tamper-evident audit trail, role-based access control and orchestrated KYC and sanctions screening - built in from day one rather than added later at extra cost.

There are no per-seat fees and no fees that grow with your assets under management. Contact our consultants for an estimate based on your specific requirements.

Wherever you need it. FinStarter is deployed in your own environment - your cloud account on AWS, Microsoft Azure or Google Cloud, in the region you choose, or on your on-premises infrastructure. You do not rent space on a shared vendor platform, and your client data never sits in a multi-tenant system alongside other firms.

This keeps data residency, security policies and access under your control, which simplifies requirements such as UK GDPR, DORA and the FCA's outsourcing rules (SYSC 8).

If you prefer not to run the infrastructure yourself, we can host and operate the platform for you as a managed service in the region of your choice.
